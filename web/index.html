    Find out where you lost time in a rally stage, and why.

    Rally Coach records your DiRT Rally 2.0 telemetry, compares it metre by metre against a reference lap of the same stage, uses a machine-learning model to find the mistakes, and explains each one in plain English.

    The pipeline

    1. CaptureThe game streams 66 values ~60 times a second over UDP. A Python logger saves every packet.
    2. CleanLaps are cut at the start and finish, restarts and reverse gear are removed, signs are calibrated, and slide angle / yaw rate are derived.
    3. CompareYour lap and the reference are resampled to the same distance grid, so every metre of the stage lines up.
    4. DetectA LightGBM model trained on real laps (with mistakes edited in), plus rules for spins and crashes, flags where you went wrong.
    5. ExplainA small LLM running on your own computer turns each flagged segment into a short coaching tip, using only the numbers it was given.

    What we record

    Driver inputs

    Throttle, brake, clutch, steering, gear

    Car motion

    Speed, position, velocity, orientation, lateral and longitudinal g, slide angle, yaw rate

    Wheels & chassis

    Wheel speed and wheelspin per wheel, suspension travel and speed per wheel, brake temperatures

    Engine

    RPM, max and idle RPM, gear count

    Stage

    Distance from start, stage time, sector and sector times, stage length

    Stage map

    Corners found from the driven path: number, direction, tightness, apex speed
